“Driven by first-principles mechanics, physical fabrication, and real-world problem solving.”

Aiden Hockinberry is an emerging mechanical engineer whose technical curiosity started early with physical assemblies and has expanded across high-performance computer simulations, CAD design in Autodesk Fusion 360, and precision additive fabrication. Rather than limiting himself to digital models or prefabricated kits, Aiden actively seeks to master the raw physics of engineering—from winding copper wire for custom brushed DC motors to operating shop machinery and learning MIG welding under professional guidance and safety precautions at Otterbein University's Point Makerspace.

He balances heavy academic coursework across high school and dual-enrollment college programs with varsity athletics and community volunteer leadership, bringing relentless curiosity, self-directed discipline, and an engineer's attention to detail to every challenge.

Electromagnetism, CAD, PC Architecture & 3D Prototyping

🌀 View 7-Disc Tesla Turbine Build →
🦀 Motorized Hexapod Kinematic Walker

Engineered an operational 6-legged walking robot implementing custom planar 4-bar linkages, driven by a continuous longitudinal transmission shaft and triple worm gearboxes. Refined through 5 hands-on design iterations: four-bar link length calibration for fluid walking, enlarged worm-mesh spur gearing for torque and backlash control, rigid metal axle replacement to prevent leg deflection, an underslung centered battery for a low roll center, and top-mounted inversion posts for bench serviceability.

🌪️ Wind-Powered Kinematic Transport

Engineered a headwind-crawling rover utilizing custom 3D-printed aerodynamic multi-blade turbine impellers, multi-stage spur gear reduction, and a 90° bevel transmission linkage to convert wind energy directly into forward mechanical drive traction.

🔄 Kinematic Geneva Drives (3-Slot & 4-Slot)

Engineered and 3D-printed two functional intermittent motion systems comparing a 4-slot ($90^\circ$ index, 1:1 motion-to-dwell ratio) and a 3-slot ($120^\circ$ index, 2:1 motion-to-dwell ratio) Geneva drive. Features tangential slot entry, a circular dwell locking brake, and a calibrated center-distance linkage fixture.

🔁 Monolithic Compliant Decision Indexer

Engineered a zero-hardware pocket-sized decision maker utilizing 3D-printed monolithic serpentine leaf spring flexures and an internal 8-tooth ratchet escapement. Converts linear thumb clicks into discrete 45° rotary stepping ($360^\circ / 8 = 45^\circ$), toggling between YES and NO states.
